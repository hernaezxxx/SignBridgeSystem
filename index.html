5-Sensor Flex Glove Matrix

The SignBridge smart glove uses five flex sensors (one per finger) connected to an Arduino microcontroller to detect hand gestures in real time.

A0
Thumb:

Flex sensor on the thumb finger

A1
Index:

Flex sensor on the index finger

A2
Middle:

Flex sensor on the middle finger

A3
Ring:

Flex sensor on the ring finger

A4
Pinky:

Flex sensor on the pinky finger

⚙️

Firmware & Hardware

The firmware is open source and available at arduino/glove_flex_sensor.ino. Compatible with Arduino Uno, Nano, and ESP32. Baud Rate: 9600.

Guide for Deaf Individuals

📱

How to Use Sign Bridge with Hearing People

This app enables you to hold your phone and instantly read everything a hearing person says in giant text:

1
Hold Phone Facing You:

Hold the phone in your hand or place it on the table facing you. The hearing person speaks towards the phone.

2
Read in Real Time:

Spoken words stream onto the screen with a glowing accent as they speak, then lock into crisp, giant white text.

3
Mic ON / OFF Button:

Use the "Mic ON / OFF" button at the top whenever you want privacy or want to pause voice capture.

4
Clear Screen Anytime:

Tap "Clear Screen" when starting a new topic. All past sentences are automatically preserved in the "History" drawer!

🎙️

External Microphone Option (for noisy places)

You can plug an external microphone into your phone for 100% voice clarity even in loud cafes or classrooms:

🎙️
Wireless Clip-on Mic

Plug receiver into phone's charging port. Clip the small wireless mic to the hearing person's collar.

🎧
Bluetooth Earbud

Pair Bluetooth earbud to phone. Speaker speaks into earbud or holds it near them.

💡

Helpful Visual Indicators

  • Sound Detection Pulse: The border glows cyan the moment voice is detected, giving you peripheral awareness.
  • Clarity Signal: Green bars mean voice is clear. Yellow bars mean the speaker should speak closer to the mic.
  • Size & Fullscreen: Tap "Fullscreen" to fill the entire phone screen without browser bars.
